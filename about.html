About ShopNow

ShopNow is a small online store for everyday tech: laptops, phones and accessories, at honest prices. This site was built as the semester project for the E-Commerce course (INT424).

How it works

  • Browse and search the catalogue (stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL database).
  • Add items to your cart, register or log in, and check out.
  • Your order and its items are saved securely; only you can see your own orders.
